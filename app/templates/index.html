Evidence

    What this means

    This view runs the interpretability stage: a small classifier over frequency-band energy, paired with a reinforcement-learning policy — trained with a GRPO-style group-relative reward — that decides which bands are worth showing as evidence. The project's Stage 1 transfer-learning classifier (Wav2Vec2) handles the primary accuracy numbers reported separately; this page is the explainability layer built on top.
